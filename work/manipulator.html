Robotic Manipulator

Overview

An open-source 5 degrees of freedom bimanual robotic arm setup designed as a low-cost platform to experiment with embodied AI.

Setup of the robotic arm.
Hardware
  • Motors: 6x ST3215 serial bus servos
  • Frame: 20x20 aluminum extrusions
  • Printed Parts: PLA/PETG (STLs in repo)
